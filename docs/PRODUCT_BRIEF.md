# OptiMode — Community Credits product brief

## 0. Executive summary
OptiMode explores an **opt-in community reward mechanism** for social platforms. A participant can enable or disable Community Credits. Under the proposed model, 70% of allocated credits go to the participating user and 30% to the platform.

## 1. Product and tagline
**OptiMode — Your participation. Your choice.**

## 2. Problem
Online communities generate value through participation, but reward structures are often opaque or inaccessible to ordinary users.

## 3. Proposed solution
An optional Community Credits layer, ideally offered as a platform integration. Users explicitly opt in. A transparent, auditable policy identifies eligible contributions and allocates credits.

## 4. Unique proposition
Opt-in rather than mandatory participation; visible allocation rules; participation by ordinary community members rather than only established creators. These are **product hypotheses**, not established competitive advantages.

## 5. Use cases
Community contributions, creator participation and platform-specific engagement programs, subject to clear anti-abuse rules.

## 6. Technical highlights
A deterministic 70/30 calculation engine is implemented as a runnable **synthetic demo**. Production would need event verification, identity and consent management, fraud controls, auditable ledgers, dispute resolution and payout compliance where applicable.

## 7. Business model and revenue potential
The 30% platform allocation is a proposed distribution rule, **not proof of profitability**. The source of credits, conversion value, operating costs, taxes and actual revenue model are unresolved.

## 8. Why explore it
A credible test is whether users value optional participation, understand the rules and trust the system enough to opt in. The next step is to validate incentives and unit economics with a narrow platform use case.

## Scope boundary
OptiMode is **not** an attention-management or notification-control application. Any earlier description of Focus/Balanced/Open modes, inboxes, notification filtering or quiet hours belongs to a separate, unrelated concept and is superseded by this brief.
